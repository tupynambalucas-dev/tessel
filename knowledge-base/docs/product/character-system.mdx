# Modular Character System (The Persona)

## 1. Concept: The Hybrid Identity
In Tessel, your character (The Persona) is the bridge between the **Social Hub** and the **3D Instances**. It is not just a game avatar; it is your digital identity across the entire ecosystem.

The system is inspired by the classic **BuddyPoke** aesthetic: expressive, highly modular "chibi" characters that allow for deep personal expression through a vast array of interchangeable parts and accessories.

## 2. Aesthetic: The Modern Chibi
- **Style**: Stylized "Chibi" (large head, small body) with clean, expressive features.
- **Expressiveness**: Characters have large, emotive eyes and mouths that can change based on the user's "Mood" or real-time voice interaction.
- **Visual Quality**: While the base geometry is optimized for web performance, the system uses high-quality WebGPU-ready materials (TSL) for effects like hair sheen, metallic accessories, and fabric textures.

## 3. Modularity: Anatomy of a Persona
The character is composed of multiple independent slots that can be customized in the Social Hub and are instantly synchronized in 3D instances.

### 3.1 Base Anatomy
- **Head**: Base head shape.
- **Body**: Base body type/skin tone.
- **Eyes**: Different iris shapes, colors, and pupil types.
- **Mouth**: Various base expressions and lip types.
- **Hair**: Modular hair pieces (Front, Back, Sides/Pony-tails).

### 3.2 Clothing
- **Top**: Shirts, jackets, hoodies.
- **Bottom**: Pants, skirts, shorts.
- **Shoes**: Sneakers, boots, sandals.
- **Outerwear**: Overcoats, capes.

### 3.3 Accessories (The "BuddyPoke" Depth)
To achieve true individuality, the Persona supports a deep accessory system:
- **Head**: Hats, beanies, headbands.
- **Face**: Glasses, masks, piercings.
- **Neck**: Necklaces, scarves, chokers.
- **Body**: Belts, backpacks.
- **Hands**: Rings (L/R), bracelets, watches.
- **Ears**: Earrings (L/R/Pair).

## 4. The Social Bridge
The character's appearance is persistent:
- **Profile Preview**: A real-time 3D render or high-quality snapshot of the character appears on the Social Hub profile.
- **Marketplace**: Users can buy, sell, or trade modular parts (limited editions, creator-made) in the Hub.
- **Instant Equipping**: Changing an accessory in the Hub reflects immediately in any active 3D Instance without requiring a re-login.

## 5. The Social-Spatial Blur (The Thin Line)

One of the core innovations of Tessel is the dissolution of the boundary between the "Social Profile" and the "3D Avatar".

- **Action Synchronization**: When a character performs an emote or changes an accessory in a 3D Instance, a "Moment" can be automatically generated on the Social Hub timeline.
- **Unified Presence**: Your "Online" status in the Hub can show exactly which 3D Instance you are in, and friends can "Jump" directly into your spatial vicinity from your social profile.
- **Identity Consistency**: The same high-fidelity shader and lighting setup used in the 3D game engine is utilized to render your profile's "Live Avatar," ensuring that you look exactly the same in the comment section as you do in the virtual plaza.
- **Mood Propagation**: If you set a "Mood" (e.g., Happy, Thinking, Bored) in the Hub, your character's base expression (eyes/mouth) and idle animations in the 3D world update automatically to reflect this state.
